# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Active Inference's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Active Inference source code for personal, research, or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Active Inference is free for noncommercial and research use.

Companion Active Inference uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Active Inference user?

If you use Companion Active Inference for personal, research, or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Active Inference?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Active Inference under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Active Inference?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Active Inference?

No. Selling Companion Active Inference, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?
